Frequently asked questions

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for? Reach out to our customer support team.

How do I upload materials?
To upload materials, simply drag and drop your files into the designated upload zone or click to select them. Supported file types include PDF, DOC, DOCX, and TXT.
Can I edit the generated content?
Yes, you can edit the generated content by clicking on the 'Chỉnh sửa' (Edit) button in the preview section. Make any necessary changes and save your updated content.
How do I save content to my library?
To save generated content to your library, click on the 'Lưu vào thư viện' (Save to Library) button at the bottom of the preview section. Your content will be stored for future use.
What types of content can I generate?
You can generate various types of content such as lesson plans, tests/exams, practice exercises, and lecture notes. Select the desired content type from the dropdown menu.
How can I access help and tutorials?
For step-by-step guides, video tutorials, and FAQs, visit the 'Trợ giúp' (Help) section in the top navigation. If you need further assistance, feel free to contact our support team.
