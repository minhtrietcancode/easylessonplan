Features

Better Document Organization

Streamline your teaching materials with intelligent organization. Keep all your lesson plans, resources, and materials perfectly structured and easily accessible whenever you need them.

All Context for LLM

Provide comprehensive context to our AI assistant. Our system understands your teaching style, curriculum requirements, and student needs to deliver personalized lesson planning support.

Auto-Generated Documents

Save hours of preparation time with automatically generated lesson plans, worksheets, and teaching materials. Our AI creates professional, curriculum-aligned documents tailored to your specific needs.

About Us

Hi, I'm Minh Triet Pham, a Data Science student at The University of Melbourne with a passion for both technology and education. After spending over two years as a mathematics tutor and witnessing firsthand how much time teachers spend on administrative tasks rather than actual teaching, I was inspired to create EasyLesson.

Having been fortunate to learn from incredible educators throughout my journey, I understand the impact that well-prepared, thoughtful lessons can have on students. EasyLesson combines my technical background in AI and data science with real classroom insights to help teachers focus on what they do best-inspiring and educating the next generation.

This tool is my way of giving back to the teaching community that has shaped who I am today.
