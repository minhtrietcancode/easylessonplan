Features

Better Document Organization

Streamline your teaching materials with intelligent organization. Keep all your lesson plans, resources, and materials perfectly structured and easily accessible whenever you need them.

All Context for LLM

Provide comprehensive context to our AI assistant. Our system understands your teaching style, curriculum requirements, and student needs to deliver personalized lesson planning support.

Auto-Generated Documents

Save hours of preparation time with automatically generated lesson plans, worksheets, and teaching materials. Our AI creates professional, curriculum-aligned documents tailored to your specific needs.
